Online Bansuri Classes FAQ

Can beginners learn Bansuri online?

Yes. Beginners can learn posture, breath, tone, fingering, and simple melodies through live online guidance.

Which Bansuri should I buy as a beginner?

A comfortable medium-sized bamboo flute is usually best. Ask Kuldeep for a scale recommendation before buying.

How long does it take to learn Bansuri?

Progress depends on consistent practice, beginning with a steady tone and moving toward melody, rhythm, and raga.

Are classes available online?

Yes. Personalized classes are available online to students worldwide.

Do you teach Hindustani classical music?

Yes. Lessons include swara, rhythm, melody, Bansuri technique, and raga-based practice.
